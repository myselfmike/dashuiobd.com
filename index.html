Core Features

Everything you need for professional OBD-II diagnostics.

From real-time PID monitoring to comprehensive datalogging, DashUI delivers accurate vehicle data through a clean, professional interface built for serious drivers.

01

Live Engine Data

Real-time PID monitoring with adaptive polling rates. Clear instrument-style cards with proper units for RPM, speed, temperature, and more.

02

DTC Read/Clear

Read diagnostic trouble codes instantly and clear them with confidence after repairs. View code descriptions for quick diagnosis.

03

CAN-BUS Support

Full support for modern OBD-II CAN protocols (ISO 15765-4, ISO 14230-4, SAE J1850). Works with 2008+ vehicles and newer.

04

Adapter Discovery

Automatic detection and pairing with popular OBD-II Bluetooth and Wi-Fi dongles. No complex manual configuration needed.

05

Datalogging

Export vehicle sensor data for analysis. Track drives, review session history, and export data for professional diagnosis.

06

Unit Flexibility

Toggle between metric and standard units. KPH/MPH, L/KPL/MPG switching with proper conversion and display formatting.

Professional Interface

Customizable, high-contrast display designed for driver visibility.

Dark theme with green signal accents inspired by diagnostic instruments. Multiple layouts, rotations, and display modes to match your vehicle and preferences. Fully optimized for phones, tablets, and folding devices with adaptive screen layouts.

Live Web Editor

Design your cluster online.

Use our browser-based editor to customize your layout before installing on your device. Drag-and-drop gauges, choose themes, and preview in real-time. Import and export configurations to share with others or backup your custom setups. Full undo/redo support ensures you can experiment freely without worry. No app installation required, just open your browser and start designing.
